Privacy Policy Refund Policy Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 1 July 2026

1. Digital Product Notice

All products sold on NEET Notes Store are digital PDF downloads delivered instantly after payment. Because of the instant, irreversible nature of digital delivery, refund requests are handled differently from physical goods.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

  • You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical error.
  • The PDF file you received is corrupted, incomplete, or does not open on any standard device.
  • You did not receive any download link or file within 24 hours of a successful payment.

3. Non-Eligible Cases

  • Change of mind after successfully downloading the notes.
  • Dissatisfaction with content depth once the file has been fully downloaded and accessed.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email support@neetnotesstore.example.com with your order ID, registered email, and a brief description of the issue. Our team typically responds within 24–48 hours.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Bundle Purchases

Refunds for the Complete NEET Bundle are evaluated as a whole; partial refunds for individual subjects within the bundle are not offered once any file has been downloaded.

7. Contact Us

For refund-related questions, reach us at support@neetnotesstore.example.com or via the Contact page.
