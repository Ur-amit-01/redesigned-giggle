1. Introduction

NEET Notes Store ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Order information: items purchased, amount paid, and payment method selected.
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, and approximate location, collected automatically for site performance.
  • Cart, wishlist, and browsing preferences stored locally in your browser to improve your shopping experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your order and deliver your purchased PDF notes.
  • To send order confirmations, download links, and customer support responses.
  • To send optional newsletter updates, only if you've subscribed.
  • To improve our website's performance, content, and product offerings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted payment processors solely to complete your transaction securely.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for checkout and do not store full payment card details on our servers.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@neetnotesstore.example.com.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your cart, wishlist, and theme preference. No third-party tracking cookies are used for advertising purposes.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us at support@neetnotesstore.example.com.
