About Us

We help NEET aspirants revise faster

NEET Notes Store was built by a small team of medical-entrance educators and toppers who believe revision should be about recall, not re-reading 900-page textbooks.

Our Story

NEET Notes Store started in 2023 when a group of NEET droppers, frustrated with bulky coaching material, began compressing their own revision notes into clean, exam-first PDFs. What started as notes shared between friends turned into a structured library used by thousands of students preparing for one of India's toughest exams.

Today, we focus on one thing: turning the entire NEET Physics and Chemistry syllabus into short, high-yield notes that respect your time in the final weeks before the exam.

Our Mission

To make high-quality, exam-focused revision material affordable and accessible to every NEET aspirant — regardless of which city, coaching institute, or budget they come from.
